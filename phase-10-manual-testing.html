⚙️ Local Environment Prerequisites
  • 1. Ensure Docker Postgres is running on port 5433 (docker compose up -d).
  • 2. Start the Vite dev server with npm run dev (runs at http://localhost:5173).
  • 3. Tournament tested: Omen AO3 (/omen-ao3) or Omen Open (/omen-open).
  • 4. Staff Login PIN: Use your SYSTEM_ADMIN_RECOVERY_PIN from .env (or set via tournament settings).

Script 1: Walk-In Competitor Flow & Score Submission

Goal: Test public competitor portal, identity disambiguation, pre-flight modal, NES Authword HUD, and score submission.
Competitor Portal

Navigate to http://localhost:5173/omen-ao3/qualify in your browser. Verify the portal landing hero and Twitch Connect card render cleanly without requiring any login.

In the Quick Dev / Mock Login card at the bottom of the portal, enter a new unique handle (e.g. TetrisWizard99) and click Mock Login.

💡 If you configured live Twitch OAuth credentials in .env, you can also click Connect with Twitch to test the real Twitch OAuth redirect.

Because TetrisWizard99 is not linked to any catalog player, the Identity Disambiguation Modal opens automatically:

  • Click the Register as New Competitor tab.
  • Verify the name field is pre-filled with TetrisWizard99.
  • Select Country (e.g. US) and Playstyle (e.g. Rolling), then click Register & Continue.
  • Verify the identity header bar displays with a Provisional New status badge.

Click Start Official Qualifier to launch the Pre-Flight modal. Verify the Confirm & Begin Official Run button is initially disabled. Check off all 4 technical requirements, then click Confirm & Begin Official Run.

Verify an 8-character max high-entropy NES Authword (e.g. STARFOX!, 1989-TET) is assigned and displayed in large monospace text.

🔄 Resilience Check: Refresh your browser (Cmd+R). Confirm your active session, assigned authword, and elapsed timer survive intact from PostgreSQL!

In the submission card:

  • Enter game scores (e.g. Game 1: 1,150,400, Game 2: 980,200).
  • Paste a Twitch VOD URL: https://twitch.tv/videos/1234567890?t=00h15m00s.
  • Click Submit Official Qualifier.
  • Verify the receipt card appears: Awaiting Judge Verification, locking further edits.

Script 2: Claim Catalog Profile & Request Technical Reset

Goal: Test claiming an existing roster competitor and triggering the stream-crash technical reset request flow.
Stream Crash & Reset

Return to /omen-ao3/qualify. In the top-right of the identity bar, click Switch Account to clear the local session.

In the mock login input, enter SharkyStream and click Mock Login.

On the Claim Existing Roster Profile tab:

  • Search for an existing catalog player (e.g. Sharky or PixelAndy).
  • Select them and click Claim Selected Profile.
  • Verify the identity bar reflects Claimed (Pending Judge Review).

Launch the run via pre-flight briefing. Once the active HUD is ticking, click ⚠️ Technical Issue / Stream Crash.

Enter a description (e.g. "OBS crashed 12 minutes in, capture card froze") and click Submit Reset Request. Verify a persistent amber banner notifies the competitor that a reset is pending judge review.

Script 3: Multi-Source Public Leaderboard Verification

Goal: Verify source indicators, VOD links, and filtering across In-Person and Online runs.
Spectator View

Click Leaderboard in the spectator navigation bar (/omen-ao3/leaderboard).

Find TetrisWizard99 in the standings table. Confirm:

  • Status pill shows [⏳ Awaiting Verification] in amber.
  • A clickable Twitch TV icon appears in their row pointing to the submitted VOD.

Click through the 4 filter pills above the leaderboard:

  • All: Displays in-person and online runs together.
  • 🏢 In-Person: Hides online competitors.
  • 🌐 Online: Isolates online competitors.
  • ⏳ Pending Review: Isolates runs awaiting judge adjudication.

Script 4: Judge Review Queue & Identity Resolution

Goal: Test staff review queue, live badge counter, adjudication modal, score editing, and technical resets.
Admin Workflow

Navigate to http://localhost:5173/omen-ao3/manage/review. If prompted, unlock with your System Admin recovery PIN.

In the left management sidebar, verify the Review Queue nav item shows a live count badge (e.g. Review Queue (2)).

Filter by Reset Requests in the table. Click Review on Sharky's row, read the crash telemetry, and click Void & Reset Run.

✅ Competitor can now return to the portal and start a clean attempt with a new authword.

Find TetrisWizard99. Click their identity badge or Resolve Identity. Choose Confirm & Promote Walk-In to bind their Twitch ID permanently to their new catalog record.

Click Review on TetrisWizard99's row. In the adjudication modal, verify the Authword and VOD link. Modify a score (e.g. change 1,150,400 to 1,162,000), add a Judge Note ("Frame matched at 15m12s"), and click Edit Score & Verify.

Open /omen-ao3/leaderboard in another tab. Confirm TetrisWizard99 now displays ✓ Verified with their updated score, and the sidebar badge decrements to Review Queue (0).

Script 5: Qualifiers Cutoff Lifecycle & Bracket Draft

Goal: Test closing qualifiers cutoff enforcement, portal locking, and draft bracket exclusion.
Cutoff & Seeding

In /omen-ao3/manage/review, click Close Qualifiers in the top header. (If any runs are unverified, a confirmation modal warns you first).

Switch to /omen-ao3/qualify. Verify the page displays a prominent Qualifiers Have Concluded banner and blocks new registrations.

Navigate to /omen-ao3/manage/brackets and click Draft Players into Tiers. Confirm verified online competitors seed cleanly into tiers based on score rank, while any DNQ, DQ, or unverified runs are excluded.

Return to /omen-ao3/manage/review and click Re-open Qualifiers to ensure two-way lifecycle toggling works cleanly.
