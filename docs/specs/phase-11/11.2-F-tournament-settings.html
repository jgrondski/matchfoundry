Batch-specific notes

  • FinalStandingsTable.tsx (≈79 sites) has delta badges whose colors depend on the delta sign. Those are static conditionals, so use cn().
  • TierManagementSection.tsx and OrgBrandPaletteSection.tsx edit theme colors: swatches render runtime colors inline; picker defaults are data.
  • settings/types.ts exports labelStyle and inputStyle (CSSProperties), imported by the four settings sections in this batch. Replace them with exported labelClass and inputClass strings and update those four importers. Other files with a local labelStyle/inputStyle of their own are unrelated.
  • store.tsx renders one toast element (≈7 sites). Convert only that JSX.
  • RoundOverridesEditor.tsx has hover handlers (guide §7).
  • The PointsThresholdsDrawer, CreateTournamentModal, VerifyBracketModal, SharedPinWarningModal and AdminFormModals shells were done in 11.1.
