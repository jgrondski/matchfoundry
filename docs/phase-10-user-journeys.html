3. Ingestion Metadata submission_source: "IN_PERSON"

🌐 Path B: Remote Online Qual
1. Self-Service Portal Player streams on Twitch, requests Authword ("ROCKET"), begins 90m timer.
2. Player Score Submission Player submits game scores, Twitch VOD link, and optional timestamps via /:slug/qualify.

is_verified: false
status: "AWAITING_VERIFICATION"
⏳ Enters Review Queue Provisional score shows on leaderboard, but locks into Review Queue for judge inspection.

⚖️ Online Judge Review Queue (/:slug/manage/review)

Notification: Review Queue (3)

The judge opens the submission drawer and completes the 3-Point Verification Checklist:

1. NES Authword: Scrubs to 10k topout on embedded VOD $\rightarrow$ confirms ROCKET was typed.
2. Timer Telemetry: Checks elapsed time (e.g. 74 mins $\le$ 90m limit).
3. Score Verification: Verifies entered scores (684k, 721k) match VOD end screens.

Adjudicator Action Buttons:

✓ Verify Qual

Scores and Authword verified. Flips badge to green Verified; permanently locks score for seeding.

✏️ Edit & Verify

Player made a transcription error (e.g., entered 721,450 but VOD was 712,450). Judge corrects score and verifies.

⚠️ Mark DNQ

Missing VOD, wrong Authword, or run over time limit. Excludes player from bracket without deleting record.

⛔ Disqualify (DQ)

Rules violation (e.g., turbo controller, emulator). Flags player profile as disqualified across tournament.

🆔 Section 3: Identity Resolution & Judge Override Hub
Entity Deduplication & Security

In competitive Tetris, Twitch handles rarely match tournament aliases (e.g. competitive name Tristan vs. Twitch handle tristandd_). MatchFoundry employs a 3-tier resolution model so players are never blocked, while judges retain absolute override authority.

🟢 Bucket 1: Verified Repeat Player

players.twitch_id = session.twitch_id

  • Recognized automatically from previous tournament.
  • Zero onboarding prompt; straight to qualify dashboard.
  • Status: VERIFIED_MATCH (100% confidence).
🟡 Bucket 2: Roster Claim (Pending)

Existing competitor with unlinked Twitch account.

  • Player selects their pre-registered profile from roster.
  • Unblocks run immediately (no waiting on judge).
  • Status: CLAIMED_PENDING_REVIEW.
🔵 Bucket 3: Provisional New Player

New walk-in competitor not yet in global database.

  • Enters alias, country, and playstyle on sign-in.
  • Can qualify immediately; score posts provisionally.
  • Status: PROVISIONAL_NEW.

⚖️ Review Queue: Identity Adjudication Controls

Judge Override Actions
Authenticated Twitch: sharky_99 (ID: 84729102)
Claimed Identity: Alex T (Pending Review)
VOD Channel: twitch.tv/sharky_99 ↗

✓ Confirm Identity Binding

Ratifies claim: writes permanent twitch_id to "Alex T" in players. Future tournaments auto-recognize them instantly!

🔄 Reassign / Link to Player

Player made a mistaken claim. Search dropdown allows judge to reassign this submission to the correct real player in the global catalog.

➕ Promote to Global Player

For new competitors: creates a canonical players record, adds them to tournament_players, and officially links their Twitch profile.

🔄 Section 4: Operational User Journeys
Operations & Spectators
⚡ Technical Reset Flow Exception Handling
Competitor Stream Crash / Technical Reset
Problem: A competitor's internet drops 15 minutes into their 90-minute window. Self-service resets are disabled to prevent abuse.
Solution Flow: The competitor clicks "Report Stream Crash / Request Reset" with an explanation note. The organizer reviews the request in the Review Queue, validates the stream abort, and clicks "Void & Issue New Authword". The competitor's portal resets cleanly with a fresh Authword and full timer.
📺 Public Spectator Flow Viewer & Broadcaster
Public Leaderboard & VOD Highlight Inspection
Spectator Experience: Fans and restream commentators visit /:slug/leaderboard. Clicking any online competitor opens their PlayerDetailDrawer with an active "Watch Qualifying VOD" button and timestamp. Commentators can pull up official runs live on stream during tournament breaks.
🔒 Tournament Director Flow Transition Gate
Qualifiers Cutoff & Bracket Lock Transition
Cutoff Sequence:
  1. Qualifying deadline arrives. Director visits /:slug/manage/qualifiers.
  2. System checks the Review Queue: if unverified submissions remain, it warns: "⚠️ 2 submissions still pending review."
  3. Once queue is clear, Director clicks "Close Qualifiers" (locking qualsClosed = true).
  4. System generates seeded draft brackets across all configured tiers (Gold, Silver, Bronze).
  5. Director reviews seeds and clicks "Lock Brackets & Begin Match Play".
